We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account and use Spendly. This includes your name, email address, password, and the financial transaction records (expenses, categories, dates, and amounts) that you input into the application.

We use the data we collect to provide, maintain, and improve Spendly, customize your experience, track your financial trends, and ensure the security of your account. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

Your data is stored securely. We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or unauthorized destruction of your data. While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@spendly.com.
